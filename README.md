# CareerGuide

CareerGuide is an AI-powered career development platform that helps professionals prepare for opportunities, improve their application materials, and make more confident career decisions. It provides personalized guidance based on a user's experience, skills, industry, and goals.

CareerGuide includes industry insights, an AI-assisted resume builder, a cover letter generator, and adaptive interview preparation with feedback and progress tracking. Together, these tools help users present their strengths clearly and take their next professional step with confidence.
